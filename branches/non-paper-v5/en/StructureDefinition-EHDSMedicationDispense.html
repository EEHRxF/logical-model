Elements defined in Ancestors:header
EHDSMedicationDispense.header 1..1 Base Dispensation header.
EHDSMedicationDispense.header.identifier 1..* Identifier Business identifier for the dispensation.
EHDSMedicationDispense.header.author[x] 1..* EHDSHealthProfessional, EHDSOrganisation, EHDSDevice Author of the document.
EHDSMedicationDispense.header.date 1..1 dateTime Date and time of issuance of the dispensation record.
EHDSMedicationDispense.header.status 1..1 CodeableConcept The status of the dispensation, e.g. completed, declined, entered-in-error.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Medication Dispense Status Codes
EHDSMedicationDispense.header.language 0..* CodeableConcept Language in which the document is written. Language is expressed by the IETF language tag.
EHDSMedicationDispense.dispenseLocation 0..1 EHDSOrganisation Location of dispense.
EHDSMedicationDispense.receiver[x] 0..1 Identification of the person who received the dispensed medicinal products, especially when it was not the patient. When not present, it is assumed that the patient is the receiver.
EHDSMedicationDispense.receiver[x].receiverEHDSPatient EHDSPatient
EHDSMedicationDispense.receiver[x].receiverEHDSHealthProfessional EHDSHealthProfessional
EHDSMedicationDispense.receiver[x].receiverEHDSRelatedPerson EHDSRelatedPerson
EHDSMedicationDispense.relatedRequest 1..* Identifier Identifier of the prescription item the dispensation is related to.
EHDSMedicationDispense.medication 1..1 EHDSMedication Exact dispensed product. If multiple medicinal products are dispensed together, multiple dispensation records shall be created.
EHDSMedicationDispense.dispensedQuantity 1..1 Quantity Number of dispensed packages if the package size is known, or number of smaller items/units, depending on the medicinal product dispensed. A unit shall be provided.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM for units of measurement, EDQM Standard Terms for units of presentation
EHDSMedicationDispense.substitutionOccurred 0..1 boolean Whether substitution was made by the dispenser. Definition of substitution is specific to the jurisdiction.
EHDSMedicationDispense.dosageInstructions 0..1 EHDSDosage Dosage and administration instructions for the dispensed medicinal product. These instructions may deviate from the instructions included in the prescription.
EHDSMedicationDispense.note 0..1 string Additional information or comments.
