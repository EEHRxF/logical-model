Logical Model: Dosage model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSDosage Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-18 Computable Name: EHDSDosage

EHDSDosage means a structured set of data elements describing the instructions for administering a medicinal product.

Usages:

  • Derived from this Logical Model: Administered dosage model
  • Use this Logical Model: Medication dispense model., Electronic prescription model and Medication use model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

EHDSDosage 0..* Base Dosage model
EHDSDosage.renderedDosageInstruction 0..1 string Full dosage information as human-readable text covering all dosage steps. Generating the text shall follow local rules and shall include all locally implemented dosage data even when such elements are not present in this model.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails 0..* Base Details of the step in a single dosage scheme.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.sequence 0..1 integer Order of the dosage instruction, if one treatment consists of several dosage steps. When two schemes share the same sequence number, they are meant to run in parallel.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.note 0..1 string Patient-oriented instructions as free text for this dosage step.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate 0..1 Base Amount of medicinal product administered per dose (= one timing).
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.dose[x] 0..1 Amount of medicinal product per one dose (1 tablet, 2-3 tablets, 20 ml).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM (units of measurement), EDQM Standard Terms (units of presentation)
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.dose[x].doseQuantity Quantity
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.dose[x].doseRange Range
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.rate[x] 0..1 Amount of medicinal product per time period (e.g. 100 mg per hour). When rate is provided per time unit, Quantity data type may be used with a suitable unit (e.g. mg/h, μg/min.).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM (units of measurement), EDQM Standard Terms (units of presentation)
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.rate[x].rateQuantity Quantity
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.doseAndRate.rate[x].rateRatio Ratio
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat 0..1 Base Repetition of the administration.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.bounds[x] 0..1 Time bounds for the treatment (current dosage scheme), Quantity contains a number of time units (e.g. 10 days), Period contains start and end date (e.g. 5.8.2023-10.8.2023).
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.bounds[x].boundsQuantity Quantity
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.bounds[x].boundsPeriod Period
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.duration 0..1 Quantity Duration of administration (e.g. ‘five minutes’, ‘one hour’).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM (units of measurement)
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.frequency 0..1 Base Frequency of intake/administration.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.frequency.numberOfTimes 0..1 integer Number of times per period (e.g. ‘three times’).
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.frequency.period 0..1 Quantity Duration to which the frequency applies (e.g. ‘… / one day’).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM (units of measurement)
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.dayOfWeek 0..* CodeableConcept The day of the week of administration, e.g. Mon., Tue.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Days Of Week
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.timeOfDay 0..* time Exact time of day of administration (e.g. ‘10:00’).
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.repeat.eventTime 0..* CodeableConcept Time period or event when the administration shall happen (‘before meal’, ‘immediately’, ‘morning’).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Event Timing
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.asNeeded 0..1 boolean Take as needed.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.bodySite 0..1 EHDSBodyStructure Body site of administration.
EHDSDosage.dosageDetails.routeOfAdministration 0..1 CodeableConcept Route of administration.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: EDQM Standard Terms

Dosage examples

Simple dosage examples

The following examples represent relatively simple dosage patterns, which are common across prescribing domain. All these patterns use only one dosage regimen. The renderedDosageInstruction is autogenerated from the structured information, and the exact algorithm may be language and implementation specific.

  1 tablet 3 times a day for 7 days 15-20ml at 8AM and 8PM from 8.12.2025 to 15.12.2025 2 tablets every third day standing on your left foot 50mg per hour intravenously for 20 minutes every Monday half a tablet as needed before breakfast
