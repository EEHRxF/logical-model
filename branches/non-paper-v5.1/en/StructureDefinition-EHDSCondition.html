Logical Model: Condition model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSCondition Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-21 Computable Name: EHDSCondition

EHDSCondition means a structured set of data elements describing a clinical condition, problem, or diagnosis.

Usages:

  • Use this Logical Model: Care plan model, Device use model, Discharge report model, Encounter model... , Imaging report model., Medical test result and report model, Medication administration model, Patient summary model, Procedure model and Service request model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

Elements defined in Ancestors:header
EHDSCondition.header
EHDSCondition.header.identifier 0..* Identifier Business identifier for the object, unique within its system. Supporting disambiguation between different contexts (systems/countries).
EHDSCondition.header.version 0..0 Version of the resource.
EHDSCondition.header.author[x] 0..* EHDSHealthProfessional, EHDSOrganisation, EHDSDevice Author of the resource.
EHDSCondition.header.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Status of the condition/problem, e.g. active, resolved, inactive.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Condition Clinical Status Codes
EHDSCondition.code 1..1 CodeableConcept Code identifying the condition, problem or diagnosis.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS (preferred), ICD-10, ICD-O, Orphacode
EHDSCondition.onsetDate 0..1 dateTime Estimated or actual date the problem/condition began.
EHDSCondition.endDate 0..1 dateTime The date or estimated date that the condition resolved or went into remission.
EHDSCondition.category 0..* CodeableConcept Category of the problem (e.g. POA – present on admission, hospital-acquired condition (HAC) and other categorisations).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSCondition.resolutionCircumstances 0..1 string Describes the reason for which the status of the problem changed from current to inactive (e.g. surgical procedure, medical treatment).
EHDSCondition.severity 0..1 CodeableConcept A subjective assessment of the severity of the condition.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSCondition.bodySite 0..* EHDSBodyStructure The anatomical location including laterality where this condition manifests itself.
EHDSCondition.stage 0..* CodeableConcept Stage or grade, usually assessed formally using a specific staging, grading or classification system, such as Tumor, Node, Metastasis (TNM), International Classification of Diseases for Oncology (ICD-O) or Breast Imaging Reporting and Data Systems (BI-RADS). Multiple assessment systems may be used.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: Not defined
EHDSCondition.diagnosisAssertionStatus 0..1 CodeableConcept Assertion about the certainty associated with a diagnosis. Evidence of condition (diagnostic, clinical or both).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Condition Verification Status
EHDSCondition.specialistContact 0..* EHDSHealthProfessional Health professional who may be specifically related to the problem, as a preferred contact.
EHDSCondition.externalResource 0..* uri External resource which may be specifically related to the problem, for example a link to a guideline on a specific rare disease problem.
EHDSCondition.note 0..1 string Free text note by the health professional.
