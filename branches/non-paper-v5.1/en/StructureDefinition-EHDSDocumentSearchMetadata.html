Logical Model: Document search metadata model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-20 Computable Name: EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata

Metadata for searching documents, based on WP v5 Annex III and the final review instructions. This search-metadata model is separate from EHDSDocument and its resource-content hierarchy.

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Specification

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata 0..* EHDSSearchMetadata Document search metadata model

Elements defined in Ancestors:subject, identifier, clinicalEventTime[x], version, author[x], date, attestation, status, language, lastUpdate
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.identifier 1..* Identifier Unique identifier of the document.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.clinicalEventTime[x] 0..0 Time or period the clinical content applies to (e.g. specimen collection time, onset of a condition, period a medication was taken).
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.version 1..1 string Version of the document.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.author[x] 1..* EHDSHealthProfessional, EHDSOrganisation, EHDSDevice Information describing who authored the resource or document.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.date 1..1 dateTime Date of finalisation/issuance of the document.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.attestation 0..* Base Document attestation details.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.attestation.attester[x] 1..1 EHDSHealthProfessional, EHDSDevice Attester who validated the document.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.attestation.dateTime 1..1 dateTime Date and time of the approval of the document by the attester.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Status of the document.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Composition Status
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.language 0..* CodeableConcept Language in which the document is written. Language is expressed by the IETF language tag.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.lastUpdate 0..1 dateTime Date and time of the last system-level update to this document (system update instant). This is technical metadata and does not represent a clinical event time.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.documentCategory 1..1 CodeableConcept Broad category of document (e.g. 11502-2 Laboratory report).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: LOINC
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.documentType 1..1 CodeableConcept Type of document (e.g. 18725-2 Microbiology studies (set)).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: LOINC
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.documentTitle 1..1 string Human-readable document title that may be displayed in search results etc. This may be documentType’s display name, or it may be assembled from multiple elements. Examples: ‘Laboratory result report’, ‘Patient summary of Jane Green 10.12.2024’.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.period 0..1 Period Time of service that is being documented.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.legalAuthentication 0..* Base Document legal authentication details. Legal authentication metadata is included only where required by applicable legal or organisational frameworks. Absence of legal authentication does not affect interoperability compliance under this profile.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x] 1..1 The person taking responsibility for the medical content of the document. legalAuthenticator may be different from author and attester (e.g author=junior doctor, attester=senior physician, legalAuthenticator= attending physician).
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x].legalAuthenticatorEHDSHealthProfessional EHDSHealthProfessional
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x].legalAuthenticatorEHDSOrganisation EHDSOrganisation
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.legalAuthentication.dateTime 1..1 dateTime Date and time when the document was authorised.
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.eventCategory 0..* CodeableConcept Categorisation of the event covered by the document (e.g. laboratory study types, imaging study types including modality). Selection of such tags or labels depends on the use case and agreement between data-sharing parties. This metadata element serves primarily for searching and filtering purposes.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS, LOINC, DICOM CID 33 Modality
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.serviceSpecialty 0..* CodeableConcept Additional details about where the content was created (e.g. clinical speciality).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.custodian 0..1 EHDSOrganisation Organisation that is in charge of maintaining the document.
