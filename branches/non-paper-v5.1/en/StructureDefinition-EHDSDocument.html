Logical Model: Document model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSDocument Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-20 Computable Name: EHDSDocument

EHDSDocument means a structured set of data elements common all health documents. Additional document search metadata is defined separately in EHDSDocumentSearchMetadata.

Usages:

  • Derived from this Logical Model: Discharge report model, Imaging report model., Medical test result and report model and Patient summary model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

EHDSDocument 0..* EHDSDataSet Document model

Elements defined in Ancestors:header
EHDSDocument.header 1..1 Base Document header.
EHDSDocument.header.identifier 1..* Identifier Unique identifier of the document.
EHDSDocument.header.version 1..1 string Version of the document.
EHDSDocument.header.author[x] 1..* EHDSHealthProfessional, EHDSOrganisation, EHDSDevice Information describing who authored the resource or document.
EHDSDocument.header.date 1..1 dateTime Date of finalisation/issuance of the document.
EHDSDocument.header.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Status of the document.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Composition Status
EHDSDocument.header.language 0..* CodeableConcept Language in which the document is written. Language is expressed by the IETF language tag.
EHDSDocument.header.documentCategory 1..1 CodeableConcept Broad category of document (e.g. 11502-2 Laboratory report).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: LOINC
EHDSDocument.header.documentType 1..1 CodeableConcept Type of document (e.g. 18725-2 Microbiology studies (set)).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: LOINC
EHDSDocument.header.documentTitle 1..1 string Human-readable document title that may be displayed in search results etc. This may be documentType’s display name, or it may be assembled from multiple elements. Examples: ‘Laboratory result report’, ‘Patient summary of Jane Green 10.12.2024’.
EHDSDocument.header.period 0..1 Period Time of service that is being documented.
EHDSDocument.header.attestation 0..* Base Document attestation details.
EHDSDocument.header.attestation.attester[x] 1..1 Attester who validated the document.
EHDSDocument.header.attestation.attester[x].attesterEHDSHealthProfessional EHDSHealthProfessional
EHDSDocument.header.attestation.attester[x].attesterEHDSDevice EHDSDevice
EHDSDocument.header.attestation.dateTime 1..1 dateTime Date and time of the approval of the document by the attester.
EHDSDocument.header.legalAuthentication 0..* Base Document legal authentication details. Legal authentication metadata is included only where required by applicable legal or organisational frameworks. Absence of legal authentication does not affect interoperability compliance under this profile.
EHDSDocument.header.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x] 1..1 The person taking responsibility for the medical content of the document. legalAuthenticator may be different from author and attester (e.g author=junior doctor, attester=senior physician, legalAuthenticator= attending physician).
EHDSDocument.header.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x].legalAuthenticatorEHDSHealthProfessional EHDSHealthProfessional
EHDSDocument.header.legalAuthentication.legalAuthenticator[x].legalAuthenticatorEHDSOrganisation EHDSOrganisation
EHDSDocument.header.legalAuthentication.dateTime 1..1 dateTime Date and time when the document was authorised.
EHDSDocument.header.eventCategory 0..* CodeableConcept Categorisation of the event covered by the document (e.g. laboratory study types, imaging study types including modality). Selection of such tags or labels depends on the use case and agreement between data-sharing parties. This metadata element serves primarily for searching and filtering purposes.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS, LOINC, DICOM CID 33 Modality
EHDSDocument.header.serviceSpecialty 0..* CodeableConcept Additional details about where the content was created (e.g. clinical speciality).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSDocument.header.custodian 0..1 EHDSOrganisation Organisation that is in charge of maintaining the document.
EHDSDocument.header.countryOfOrigin 0..1 CodeableConcept Country of origin of the document.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
EHDSDocument.presentedForm 0..* EHDSAttachment A narrative easy-to-read representation of the full data set, e.g. PDF-version of a document.
