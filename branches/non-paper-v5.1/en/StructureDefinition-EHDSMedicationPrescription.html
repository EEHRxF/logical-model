EHDSMedicationPrescription 0..* EHDSDataSet Electronic prescription model

Elements defined in Ancestors:header
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header 1..1 Base Prescription header.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.subject 1..1 EHDSPatient The person for whom a medicinal product is prescribed/ordered.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.identifier 1..* Identifier Business identifier(s) for the prescription.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.version 0..0 Version of the resource.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.author[x] 1..* EHDSHealthProfessional Author of the document.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.date 1..1 dateTime Date and time of issuance (signature) of the prescription by prescriber.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Status of authorisation for dispensing the prescription items. For a multiple-item prescription, this conveys the aggregate status of the entire prescription. In the case of a single-item prescription, prescriptionItem.status shall be the same as header.status.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Medication Request Status
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.language 0..* CodeableConcept Language in which the document is written. Language is expressed by the IETF language tag.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.statusReason[x] 0..1 Reason for the current status of prescription, for example the reason why the prescription was cancelled or changed from a previous state.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: Not defined
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.statusReason[x].statusReasonCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.header.statusReason[x].statusReasonString string
EHDSMedicationPrescription.presentedForm 0..* EHDSAttachment A narrative easy-to-read representation of the full dataset, e.g. PDF version of a document.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem 1..* Base Prescription line for one medicinal product. If multiple medicinal products are prescribed, all items need to be authored together.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.identifier 1..* Identifier Identifier for a single item on the prescription. This identifier may be linked to the prescription identifier. In the case of a multiple-line prescription, this identifier may be constructed, for example, as the prescription identifier combined with the line number.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.status 1..1 CodeableConcept Status of a single item on prescription. In the case of a single-item prescription, the status of the prescription has the same meaning as the status of the item.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Medication Request Status
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.statusReason[x] 0..1 Reason for the current status of prescription item, for example the reason why the prescription was cancelled or why the prescription was changed from the previous status.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: Not defined
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.statusReason[x].statusReasonCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.statusReason[x].statusReasonString string
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.medication 1..1 EHDSMedication Prescribed medicinal product.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.indication[x] 0..* Reason for the prescription (typically diagnosis, or a procedure).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS (preferred), ICD-10, Orphacode
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.indication[x].indicationCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.indication[x].indicationString string
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.intendedUseType 0..1 CodeableConcept Intent of the prescription – prophylaxis, treatment, anaesthesia, etc.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.periodOfUse 0..1 Period Period over which the medicinal product is to be taken (in the case of multiple dosage schemes, this shall be the overall period of all dosages).
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.quantityPrescribed 1..1 Quantity Overall quantity of medicinal product prescribed in this prescription item (e.g. number of packages or number of tablets). Where the total quantity cannot be determined unambiguously at issuance, the prescriber may express it on the basis of dosage instructions, validity period and number of repeats.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: UCUM for units of measurement, EDQM Standard Terms for units of presentation
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.dosageInstructions 0..1 EHDSDosage Dosage and administration instructions.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.validityPeriod 0..1 Period The period over which the prescription is considered valid and dispensable. validityPeriod.start is, if missing, assumed to be the date of issuance of the prescription.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution 0..1 Base Whether and, if so, which type of substitution is allowed, as indicated by the prescriber, for this prescription. The legislation in the country of dispensation applies for performing a substitution.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.allowed[x] 0..1 Whether and, if so, to what extent substitution is allowed.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Substance Admin Substitution
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.allowed[x].allowedBoolean boolean
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.allowed[x].allowedCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.reason[x] 0..1 Reason for the substitution requirement (e.g. Biological product, Patient allergic to an excipient in alternative products).
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.reason[x].reasonCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.substitution.reason[x].reasonString string
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.numberOfRepeats 0..1 integer Number of refills authorised – how many times the prescription item may be dispensed in addition to the original dispensation. If the element is missing, no repeats are authorised.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.minimumDispenseInterval 0..1 Quantity Minimum dispensation interval. If a prescription allows for repeated dispensations, the interval between dispensations shall be stated here.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.offLabel 0..1 Base Indicates that the medicinal product is knowingly prescribed for use for an age group, dosage or route of administration not included in the authorised product information.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.offLabel.isOffLabelUse 1..1 boolean Indicates whether the medicinal product is prescribed outside the terms of its marketing authorisation. The value shall be ‘true’ if the medicinal product is prescribed for off-label use and ‘false’ otherwise. Where a reason for off-label use is provided, the value shall be ‘true’.
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.offLabel.reason[x] 0..1 Reason or related clarification for off-label use.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: Not defined
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.offLabel.reason[x].reasonString string
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.offLabel.reason[x].reasonCodeableConcept CodeableConcept
EHDSMedicationPrescription.prescriptionItem.note 0..1 string Additional information or comments, e.g. message to the dispenser.
