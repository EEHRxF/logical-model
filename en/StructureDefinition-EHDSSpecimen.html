Logical Model: Specimen model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSSpecimen Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-20 Computable Name: EHDSSpecimen

EHDSSpecimen means a structured set of data elements describing a sample collected.

Usages:

  • Use this Logical Model: Imaging report model., Imaging study model., Medical test result and report model, Observation model... , Service request model and Specimen model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

EHDSSpecimen.identifier 1..* Identifier An identifier of the specimen which is unique within a defined scope.
EHDSSpecimen.status 0..1 CodeableConcept Availability of the specimen.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Specimen Status
EHDSSpecimen.type 0..1 CodeableConcept The kind of material that forms the specimen.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSSpecimen.specimenSource[x] 0..1 The source where the specimen came from. This may be from a patient, from a location (e.g. the source of an environmental sample) or from a device.
EHDSSpecimen.specimenSource[x].specimenSourceEHDSPatient EHDSPatient
EHDSSpecimen.specimenSource[x].specimenSourceEHDSLocation EHDSLocation
EHDSSpecimen.specimenSource[x].specimenSourceEHDSDevice EHDSDevice
EHDSSpecimen.parentSpecimen 0..* EHDSSpecimen Specimen from which this specimen originated.
EHDSSpecimen.request 0..* EHDSServiceRequest Service request on the basis of which the specimen was collected.
EHDSSpecimen.collection 0..* Base Collection details.
EHDSSpecimen.collection.performer[x] 0..1 Person or organisation who collected the specimen.
EHDSSpecimen.collection.performer[x].performerEHDSHealthProfessional EHDSHealthProfessional
EHDSSpecimen.collection.performer[x].performerEHDSOrganisation EHDSOrganisation
EHDSSpecimen.collection.performer[x].performerEHDSPatient EHDSPatient
EHDSSpecimen.collection.performer[x].performerEHDSRelatedPerson EHDSRelatedPerson
EHDSSpecimen.collection.collected[x] 1..1 The period or date and time of specimen collection.
EHDSSpecimen.collection.collected[x].collectedPeriod Period
EHDSSpecimen.collection.collected[x].collectedDateTime dateTime
EHDSSpecimen.collection.quantity 0..1 Quantity The quantity of specimen collected.
EHDSSpecimen.collection.method 0..1 CodeableConcept If relevant for the results, the method of obtaining the specimen.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
EHDSSpecimen.collection.device 0..1 EHDSDevice Device used to perform specimen collection.
EHDSSpecimen.collection.bodySite 0..1 EHDSBodyStructure Anatomic location where the material is collected.
EHDSSpecimen.receivedDate 0..1 dateTime Date and time when specimen is received by the testing laboratory.
EHDSSpecimen.container 0..* Base The container holding the specimen.
EHDSSpecimen.container.specimenQuantity 0..1 Quantity Quantity of specimen within the container.
EHDSSpecimen.container.containerDevice 1..1 EHDSDevice The device for the container holding the specimen.
EHDSSpecimen.condition 0..* CodeableConcept State of the specimen. State of the specimen shall be aligned with the dataAbsentReason element of the Observation model.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: HL7 Specimen Condition
EHDSSpecimen.note 0..1 string Any details or issues about the specimen or during the specimen collection (for example: broken vial, sent with patient, frozen).
