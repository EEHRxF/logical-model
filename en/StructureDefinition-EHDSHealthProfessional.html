Logical Model: Health professional model

Official URL: http://ehds.eu/fhir/models/StructureDefinition/EHDSHealthProfessional Version: 0.3.0
Draft as of 2026-11-20 Computable Name: EHDSHealthProfessional

EHDSHealthProfessional means a structured set of data elements describing a health professional.

Usages:

  • Use this Logical Model: Condition model, DataSet model, Discharge report model, Document model... , Document search metadata model, Encounter model, Imaging report model., Immunisation model, Medical test result and report model, Medication dispense model., Electronic prescription model, Observation model, Procedure model, Data group search metadata model and Specimen model

You can also check for usages in the FHIR IG Statistics

NameFlagsCard.TypeDescription & Constraints    Filter:



EHDSHealthProfessional 0..* Base Health professional model
EHDSHealthProfessional.identifier 0..* Identifier An identifier of the health professional that is unique within a defined scope. Example: national health professional ID. Multiple identifiers may be provided.
EHDSHealthProfessional.name 0..1 EHDSHumanName Name of the health professional.
EHDSHealthProfessional.address 0..* EHDSAddress Address of the health professional.
EHDSHealthProfessional.telecom 0..* EHDSTelecom Telecommunication contact information (address) associated with the health professional, such as phone number, email or messaging service.
EHDSHealthProfessional.professionalRole 0..* Base Professional role that the health professional is practising in a given organisation.
EHDSHealthProfessional.professionalRole.role 0..* CodeableConcept Health professional role.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS (preferred), ISCO-08
EHDSHealthProfessional.professionalRole.organisation 0..1 EHDSOrganisation The organisation where this role is available.
EHDSHealthProfessional.professionalRole.specialty 0..* CodeableConcept The speciality of the health professional they are practising in the organisation.
Binding Description: (required) ⚠: SNOMED GPS
